# Journal figure specifications

Always check the target journal's current author guide before final submission; individual journals can override their publisher's defaults. Values below were checked against publisher guidance in November 2026.

## Elsevier
Remote Sensing of Environment, ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation (JAG), Landscape and Urban Planning, Science of the Total Environment, Environment International, Computers, Environment and Urban Systems.

| Width | mm | pt |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal | 30 | 85 |
| Single column | 90 | 255 |
| 1.5 column | 140 | 397 |
| Double column (full width) | 190 | 539 |

Resolution: 300 dpi for halftones (photos, satellite images), 500 dpi for combination art (images with text or lines), 1000 dpi for line art. Vector EPS/PDF preferred for line art. Lettering about 7 pt at final size, no smaller than 6 pt for subscripts.

Source: Elsevier, "Artwork sizing" and "Artwork types", elsevier.com/authors/policies-and-guidelines/artwork-and-media-instructions.

## IEEE
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (TGRS), IEEE JSTARS, IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (GRSL).

| Width | inches | mm | picas |
|---|---|---|---|
| One column | 3.5 | 88.9 | 21 |
| Two columns | 7.16 | 182 | 43 |

Resolution: at least 300 dpi for colour and greyscale, at least 600 dpi for black-and-white line art. Accepted formats include PS, EPS, PDF, PNG and TIFF. Avoid figures narrower than one column.

Source: Proceedings of the IEEE, "Guidelines for figures and tables"; IEEE Author Center, "Resolution and Size".

## Taylor & Francis
International Journal of Geographical Information Science (IJGIS), International Journal of Digital Earth (IJDE), International Journal of Remote Sensing (IJRS), Annals of GIS.

Taylor & Francis does not fix one column width for all journals. Use at least 300 dpi for colour and greyscale and 600–1200 dpi for line art, and size to the journal's text block. Check the specific journal's "Instructions for authors".

## MDPI
Remote Sensing, ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information, Land.

Single-column layout. Prepare figures at the text width and at least 300 dpi (1000 dpi for line art). Check the journal's instructions page.

## Quick conversion

```
pixels = dpi × width_in_inches        1 inch = 25.4 mm
90 mm  at 300 dpi ≈ 1063 px            190 mm at 300 dpi ≈ 2244 px
88.9 mm at 600 dpi = 2100 px           182 mm at 300 dpi ≈ 2150 px
```
